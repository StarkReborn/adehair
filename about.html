Our story, our team, and our passion for beautiful hair

Our Story

Ade Hair & Beauty began its journey 10 years ago with a simple vision—to create a salon where style meets care, and every client feels celebrated. What started as a small dream has grown into a trusted name in the heart of the UK, known for bringing out the natural beauty in everyone who walks through our doors.

From day one, our mission has been more than offering hair and beauty services. We believe in creating an experience that blends skill, creativity, and warmth. Over the years, we’ve built lasting relationships with our clients, many of whom feel more like family than customers.

Our team is passionate about keeping up with the latest trends while honouring timeless techniques. Whether it’s a fresh hairstyle, a bold new look, or a relaxing treatment, we tailor every service to reflect your unique personality and lifestyle.

A decade on, Ade Hair & Beauty remains rooted in the values it was founded on: quality, trust, and care. We are proud of how far we’ve come and excited for the future, continuing to help our community look and feel their absolute best.
